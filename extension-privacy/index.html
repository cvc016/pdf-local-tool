繁體中文

English

  • All PDF and image processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.
  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal or usage data. No analytics, tracking, or ads.
  • Only stored locally in your browser (localStorage): your license key, the time of its last successful check, and a daily free-usage counter. Removing the extension deletes them.
  • The only network connection is to api.gumroad.com: when you enter a Gumroad license key (and roughly every 7 days afterwards), the extension sends the license key and this product's product_id to Gumroad (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify) to confirm the purchase. After activation it works offline; it must reach Gumroad at least once every 30 days.

  • The only host permission requested is https://api.gumroad.com/*, used solely for license verification. No other Chrome permissions.
